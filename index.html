Nursing Home Neglect

Some of the Results of Nursing Home Neglect

Nursing homes are often filled with underpaid staff members who distribute medications, assist with personal hygiene, and help with other needs. Quite often, residents of nursing homes and assisted living facilities become victims of their environments.

  • Starvation
  • Dehydration
  • Emotional abuse
  • Financial abuse
  • Over-medication
  • Unauthorized medication
  • Unnecessary or dangerous medication, including opioid prescriptions

Signs of Neglect & Abuse

What Neglect and Abuse Can Look Like

Have you or a loved one suffered from any of the following?

Bedsores & Pressure Ulcers

Bedsores and painful pressure sores, including pressure ulcers.

Falls, Fractures & Broken Bones

Falls, fractures, and broken bones suffered in the facility's care.

Dehydration & Malnutrition

Dehydration, malnutrition, and starvation from neglect.

Medication Errors

Medication errors — over-medication, unauthorized medication, and unnecessary or dangerous medication, including opioid prescriptions.

Physical Abuse & Assault

Physical abuse, assault and battery, and unreasonable physical restraint.

Sexual Abuse

Sexual abuse of a resident by staff or others in the facility.

Infections & Sepsis

Infections, including sepsis, that proper care should have prevented.

Head & Unexplained Injuries

Head injuries and unexplained injuries, and failure to protect residents from health and safety hazards.

Wrongful Death

Wrongful death of a loved one from nursing home abuse, neglect, or negligence.
